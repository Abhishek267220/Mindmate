Student Tips 💡

Simple habits that make a big difference.

⏰

Study in Blocks

Study for 25 minutes, then take a 5-minute break. This is called the Pomodoro Technique!

💧

Stay Hydrated

Drink at least 8 glasses of water a day. Your brain works better when properly hydrated.

📝

Write Notes by Hand

Handwriting notes helps your brain remember information better than copy-pasting.
